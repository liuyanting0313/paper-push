1. E2DRL: End-to-End Differentiable Reinforcement Learning for 3D Obstacle Avoidance in Quadruped Robots

四足机器人在真实环境里靠视觉绕开障碍一直不好做，因为一碰到障碍就结束训练、限制太硬，会让学习过程卡住、学不好。这篇论文把碰撞和提前结束改成更平缓的惩罚和引导，让机器人从看到画面到做出避障动作整套一起学；好处是训练更省、能更好保持设定速度并绕开三维障碍，而且已经在真实四足机器人上验证可行。

AI 精读要点 · DeepSeek

创新点: 提出 E2DRL，用多身体关键点穿透惩罚和速度条件 stop-gradient 塑形构造软可微障碍交互，使端到端可微强化学习可用于四足机器人三维视觉避障。

方法: 在可微仿真器中以 SAPO 训练特权教师策略，经 DAgger 蒸馏为基于深度的循环学生策略，再通过地形感知门控与基础运动策略融合用于部署。

结论: 仿真中样本效率高于 PPO，速度跟踪与三维避障优于 PPO 和 Duality-based DCBF NMPC，并在 Unitree Go2 静态和动态环境中完成首例 sim-to-real 验证。

Abstract

Differentiable reinforcement learning shows strong promise for robot policy learning, but its application to legged locomotion has remained limited to blind walking or simulated 2D tasks. Extending it to end-to-end visual obstacle avoidance is challenging because hard collision constraints and early termination upon contact corrupt first-order gradients, trapping optimizers in local minima. We present E2DRL, which addresses this with a soft differentiable obstacle-interaction formulation: penetration penalties across multiple body keypoints with velocity-conditioned stop-gradient shaping to provide the first-order training signals for 3D obstacle avoidance. To realize a deployable perception-control system, a privileged teacher trained via Soft Analytic Policy Optimization (SAPO) in a differentiable simulator is distilled into a recurrent depth-based student via DAgger, then blended with a base locomotion policy using a terrain-aware gating module for deployment. In simulation, E2DRL achieves higher sample efficiency than PPO and demonstrates superior velocity-tracking performance and 3D obstacle-avoidance capability compared with both PPO and Duality-based DCBF NMPC. We present the first sim-to-real validation of end-to-end differentiable RL for 3D obstacle avoidance on a Unitree Go2 in static and dynamic environments.

Abstract

Accurate trajectory tracking is essential for autonomous underwater vehicles operating with uncertain hydrodynamics and limited thruster authority. This paper develops a low-complexity adaptive prescribed performance controller for uncertain underwater vehicle dynamics with coupled and saturated inputs. The proposed method adapts the performance functions directly through the mismatch between the requested and saturated control inputs, enlarging the performance funnels only when the available actuation cannot meet the nominal specifications. A coupled invariant-set analysis establishes boundedness of all closed-loop signals, prescribed bounds on the filtered tracking errors, and explicit feasibility conditions. Comparative simulations on a BlueROV2 underwater vehicle show that the adaptive design maintains prescribed performance during a figure-eight maneuver, whereas conventional prescribed performance control loses feasibility under input limits. A real-time pool experiment further demonstrates stable trajectory tracking and the practical implementation of the proposed method on an underwater robotic platform.

3. LAG-IO: Multi-IMU State Estimation for Legged Robots Using Factor Graphs

大白话人话速读 · DeepSeek

四足机器人在坑洼、容易打滑的路面上行走时，如果只靠身体里的一个运动传感器和腿部动作来推算自己的位置，很容易越算越偏，尤其脚打滑或动作剧烈时更明显。这篇论文把身体和每条腿上的多个运动传感器以及关节转动信息放在一起综合判断，还会根据脚是否稳稳踩地来动态调整信任程度。这样做的实际好处是位置估计更准，上下方向不容易乱飘，脚接触不稳定时轨迹也更顺滑。

AI 精读要点 · DeepSeek

创新点: 提出LAG-IO，一种基于因子图紧耦合主体IMU、多腿IMU与关节编码器的滑动窗口状态估计框架，并引入基于核密度估计的连续概率接触检测来自适应缩放零速更新因子、构造运动学相对位置因子锚定各腿惯性状态。

方法: 在滑动窗口因子图中融合主体IMU、多腿IMU和关节编码器测量，利用核密度估计检测足地接触并调节零速更新协方差，同时加入腿与主体间的运动学相对位置因子。

结论: 四足机器人多地形实验表明，相较现有基线，LAG-IO平面轨迹更准确、垂直发散更小、不稳定接触下轨迹更平滑，并保持有竞争力的姿态可观性。

Abstract

Robust proprioceptive state estimation is crucial for legged robots, particularly in challenging environments where exteroceptive sensors are unreliable. While traditional leg odometry typically relies on a single body-mounted inertial measurement unit (IMU) fused with leg kinematics via an extended Kalman filter (EKF), such methods are vulnerable to linearization errors and hard-contact assumptions, leading to severe drift during slippage or dynamic locomotion.In this paper, we propose LAG-IO, a factor graph-based multi-IMU state estimation framework that tightly couples measurements from a main body IMU, multiple leg-mounted IMUs, and joint encoders within a sliding-window optimization. To handle non-stationary foot-ground interactions, we introduce a continuous probabilistic contact detection module based on kernel density estimation (KDE), which adaptively scales the covariance of zero-velocity update (ZUPT) factors. Furthermore, kinematic relative position factors are formulated to structurally anchor the independent leg inertial states to the robot's main body. Experimental results on a quadruped robot across various unstructured terrains demonstrate that, compared to existing baselines, LAG-IO achieves higher planar trajectory accuracy, reduces unobservable vertical divergence, and provides smoother trajectory transitions during unstable contacts, while maintaining competitive attitude observability.

这篇论文想解决的是：机器人在某些方向上测量很不清楚、数据很差时，估计自己的位置和姿态容易出错，现有办法要么算不动，要么不能保证结果可靠。他们想出的办法是先给出候选答案，再用一套快速检查办法判断这个答案靠不靠谱，而不是硬去直接算那个特别难的问题。好处是即使测量条件很弱，也能很快判断候选答案是否可信，减少把正确答案误判掉，在测量很差的画面匹配任务中更稳。

AI 精读要点 · DeepSeek

创新点: 提出一种在观测退化下仍可进行全局认证的候选驱动对偶认证框架，避免半定松弛的秩扩张并保持代价紧性与乘子恢复。

方法: 从epigraph视角证明退化使原始最优集成为平坦等代价面但代价紧性和乘子恢复不受影响，并结合解重映射的观测感知局部搜索、闭式对偶乘子恢复以及基于可观测Hessian最小非零特征值和残余泄漏的自适应对偶安全阈值。

结论: 在退化测量下的鲁棒配准中验证了该框架可在不直接求解半定规划的情况下实现快速全局认证，乘子恢复达亚微秒级，并降低弱可观测条件下最优候选的误拒。

Abstract

Certifiable optimization provides global optimality guarantees for non-convex state estimation in robotics. However, under severe physical sensing degeneracy, convex Semidefinite Programming relaxations solved via Interior-Point Methods suffer from rank expansion, rendering direct semidefinite solving and spectral rounding algebraically invalid. In this paper, we establish a universal candidate-driven dual certification framework that resolves this challenge without incurring rank expansion. First, from an epigraph perspective, we prove that while sensing degeneracy expands the primal optimal set into a flat equi-cost face, cost tightness and multiplier recovery remain strictly intact, enabling global verification without direct semidefinite solving. Second, we propose an observability-aware local search using solution remapping, coupled with a closed-form dual multiplier recovery scheme that executes in sub-microsecond latency. Third, we derive a model-specific adaptive dual safety threshold parameterized by the minimum non-zero eigenvalue of the observable Hessian and uneradicated residual leakage, preventing false rejections of optimal candidates under weak observability. We validate our universal framework with robust registration under degenerate measurements.

Abstract

Distribution-system state estimation is a growing necessity for the modern power grid as the addition of local storage and generation add system complexity that an operator or aggregator is not able to directly monitor. Classical methods work well when all system elements are time-synchronized and at least partly observable, but multi-rate monitoring of sparse heterogeneous sensors typically relies on machine learning approaches. It is a common practice in the literature to structure this task as a two-stage pipeline, first fusing data from disparate sensors and then feeding the fused pseudo-measurements to a separately trained state estimator. We propose a heterogeneous latent graph ODE in which measurement functions and sampling processes are typed and localized on specific buses, paired with a graph- transformer state estimator solving DSSE specifically for multi-rate heterogeneous sensor placements, and demonstrate its effectiveness across multiple IEEE standard topologies as observability degrades.

Abstract

Abstract

Real-time monocular human pose estimation is an important sensing component for human-centered intelligent systems. While recent camera-based methods have achieved impressive accuracy, fundamental challenges persist, particularly when visual observations are incomplete, such as during lower-body occlusion or short out-of-frame intervals. In these challenging but common scenarios, vision-only systems often struggle to reconstruct physically plausible lower-limb motion and reliable foot-ground contact. Due to the highly unobtrusive and wearable nature of smart insoles, this paper investigates whether simple plantar pressure and acceleration sensors can effectively resolve these visually induced limitations. To systematically investigate this, we first collect a synchronized multimodal dataset and then propose a part-decoupled temporal fusion framework. The proposed model fuses foot-sensing features into the lower-body branch to compensate for incomplete visual evidence, while a lightweight coordinator exchanges information between the upper- and lower-body temporal representations to maintain whole-body consistency. The framework is evaluated with three representative real-time visual backbones based on 2D keypoints, 3D landmarks, and SMPL-based human mesh recovery. We evaluate our framework on three representative real-time visual backbones. Quantitative experiments demonstrate that incorporating simple foot sensors consistently improves pose accuracy, motion smoothness, and gait-related measurements over vision-only baselines, with the most significant gains observed under occlusion and short visual-dropout conditions. Ultimately, our results demonstrate that unobtrusive foot sensing provides vital and complementary physical observability when camera observations are temporarily unavailable.

8. Design of Privacy-Preserving Estimators Under Prior Information Exposure

作者: Le Liu, Yu Kawano, Ming Cao

这篇论文想解决的是：系统对外发布运行状态结果时，可能把本来不该公开的敏感信息带出去，尤其当外部的人事先已经掌握了一些背景信息时，现有做法没有充分考虑这种风险。作者想出的办法是：在发布结果里主动加入经过专门设计的干扰，并同时调整计算方式，让发布的数据仍然有用，但更难反推出敏感信息；他们用电力系统模拟说明，这种做法能在保护隐私和保持结果准确之间取得可调的平衡。

创新点: 在考虑对手先验信息暴露的场景下，提出基于点对最大泄露的隐私保护状态估计框架，并揭示卡尔曼滤波在无释放噪声时隐私泄露最大，进而给出隐私约束下的估计误差下界与联合设计方法。

方法: 针对离散时间线性随机系统，以PML刻画隐私，推导满足(ε,δ)-PML的释放噪声条件，并将估计器增益与释放噪声协方差联合设计转化为凸半定规划。

结论: 仿真在源荷电力系统上验证了该方法可实现隐私与估计效用之间的可调权衡，并在保证PML隐私的同时给出稳态估计误差上界。

Abstract
